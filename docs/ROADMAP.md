- Authentication/RBAC
- Vehicle catalog, price and stock
- CRM leads and follow-up
- Dashboard and reports

## Phase 2 — AI CS
- Knowledge base/RAG
- MySQL-aware price and stock responses
- Lead capture and handoff to sales

## Phase 3 — Telegram
- Text webhook
- Voice -> speech-to-text -> AI CS
- Sales notifications

## Phase 4 — Marketing Studio
- Caption/headline/hashtag generator
- AI image prompt and image generation provider
- Content calendar and approval workflow

## Phase 5 — Production
- Nginx + HTTPS
- Backup and monitoring
- RBAC/audit hardening
- VPS deployment and CI/CD
